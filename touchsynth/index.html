TouchSynth

A multitouch XY-pad MPE synthesizer. The VST3 runs in your DAW; the APK turns your Android tablet into the controller. Connect them with a USB-C cable.

Protocol v1.1 · MPE Lower Zone · 15 voices · ±48 semitone pitch bend · CC74 Y axis
